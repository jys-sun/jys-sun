## Hi there 👋

I am Jiayi, a PhD student at the University of Toronto, where I am a member of [forcolab](https://www.eecg.utoronto.ca/~shuruiz/forcolab/index.html) and the Programming Languages and Software Engineering group ([PROSE](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~prose/)).

My research focuses on LLMs and AI coding agents for scientific and open-source software. I study how agents can write scientifically correct code that reflects developers’ intentions and learn from their failures. I’m also interested in HCI and causal inference to understand how AI tools affect developer productivity and software sustainability.

Outside my research, I enjoy tinkering with formal verification, training and fine-tuning small language models, writing shaders on [Shadertoy](https://www.shadertoy.com/), and applying reinforcement learning for fun projects with open-source bipedal and hexapod robots.
